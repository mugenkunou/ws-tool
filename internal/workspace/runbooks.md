# Runbooks

How to do a task here: the route, its order and the judgment calls. A runbook is not a script. The blocks (scripts, specs, configs) do the work, `WISDOM.md` keeps the lessons, and an entry says which blocks to use, in what order, and what to decide on the way.

A run starts by reading the table. Pick the row whose task means what was asked, even in other words, then follow its entry below the table. The format of an entry is in workspace-write's `write-rules.md`, under `references/RUNBOOKS.md`.
